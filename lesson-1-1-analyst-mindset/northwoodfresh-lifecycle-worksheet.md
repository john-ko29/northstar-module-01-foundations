**Analyst:** John Ko

**Client:** NorthwoodFresh Grocery
**Stakeholder:** Sven Anderson, CFO

## 1. Define

The question: Why has the customer counts at the flagship downtown Seattle location have been dropping in the last 6 months while other locations are either stable or growing. 

The decision this will inform:  Which direction the CEO should take: change management, close, or adjust prices/hours/programs.

What "done" looks like: Majority of the drop can be identified with high confidence level and has recommended ways to handle the drop. 

## 2. Collect

Datasets needed:  Revenue Numbers, Customer Review, Transaction, Campaign/Promo Numbers, Loyalty Members, Nearby Competitor Locations, Competitor Revenue Numbers, Downtown Seattle Traffic Numbers, When Customers Numbers Dropped

Datasets that already exist within Brewbird's systems: Revenue Numbers, Customer Review, Transaction, Campaign/Promo Numbers, Loyalty Members

Datasets that already exist from external Sources: Nearby Competitor Locations, Competitor Revenue Numbers, Downtown Seattle Traffic Numbers

Datasets that don't exist or aren't accessible: When Customers Numbers Dropped

## 3. Clean

1. Reconciling product codes across the warehouse system and the point-of-sale system
2. Reconciling competitors that are closeby and their revenue to the past 6 months
3. Creating fair comparison between all 32 stores. Make sure all data at a similiar time for different timezones. 

## 4. Analyze

1. Why did the decline happen and what shape of the decline took place
2. Is there a pattern of how the decline happens
3. How much of the revenue is loyalty members
4. How did competitors nearby do
5. Was the traffic of the store consisent with previous timelines
6. Has customer reviews change at all
7. Was a huge cost such as renovation that happen during the last 6 months
8. Any promos or deals that happen/stop during this time

## 5. Visualize

1. A chart that contains a 6 months that include the the flagship and 31 other location with median, and traffic
2. charts of competitors and flagship location revenue for the last 6 months
3. show sales of loyaly members vs regular customers

## 6. Communicate

The flagship is losing non-loyal members to competitors. While there are increase in revenue from new customers in our competitors. Having promos that target new customers or non-member customers may may help increase the flagship revenue.

## 7. Act

1. Capture for next few months how much customers we have compared to the last few months
2. See if our non-loyal revenues ratio increase compared to the last 6 months
3. Follow up with the CEO to see 1 month after the report
